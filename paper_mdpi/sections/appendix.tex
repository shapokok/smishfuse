\section[\appendixname~\thesection]{Full Tables and Additional Figures}\label{app:full}

This appendix gives the results of all models that are summarised in Section~\ref{sec:results}: the clean test sets (Table~\ref{tab:main}), the settings of the published methods (Table~\ref{tab:settings}), obfuscation and ablations (Table~\ref{tab:robustness}), low data and transfer (Table~\ref{tab:lowdata} and Figure~\ref{fig:learning}), efficiency and cost (Table~\ref{tab:efficiency}), every comparison of Full with the best baseline (Table~\ref{tab:significance}), the tests of Lite and Full against each re-implemented published method (Table~\ref{tab:pubtests}) and the unmerged list of the strongest n-grams of Lite (Table~\ref{tab:ngrams-full}). Figures~\ref{fig:pr} and \ref{fig:confusion} show the precision-recall curves and the confusion matrix of Full, and Figure~\ref{fig:adaptation} the adaptation curve of the Kazakhstan case study.
